2.- Instrument description and interpretation criteria

This item bank measures seven domains grouped into three institutional clusters. Each value is the % correct for that subtopic within the attempt. On page 1 (Psychology criterion): IRZ is the average of the percentages for fluid reasoning and quantitative reasoning; IME groups the mean of working memory, processing speed, and associative memory / retrieval; ICR groups the mean of verbal comprehension and visual-spatial processing. G.I. is accuracy across all items in the bank — it does not sum the three group indices or average them. Below those three blocks on the first page appears the arithmetic mean of IRZ, IME, and ICR; it differs from G.I. and from the IQ number.
