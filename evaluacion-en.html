Emotional Intelligence Assessment

Effective management of emotions is a key factor for personal and professional success. This assessment is designed so that adults can evaluate their development level in certain key emotional skills.

The instrument identifies 12 emotional skills and 2 areas related to interpersonal communication style.

The assessment includes 244 questions and can provide a diagnosis of emotional intelligence management across four areas:

  • Personal Development.
  • Interpersonal Relationships.
  • Life Skills.
  • Interpersonal Communication.
