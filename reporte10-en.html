2. Instrument description and interpretation guidelines

Results are reported on a 1–100 scale, equivalent to a percentile-style score. This shows whether the person falls below, at, or above the age-matched reference group.
Three interpretation ranges are used for readability:
1–40 · Low 41–70 · Moderate 71–100 · High
Red highlights skills to develop, yellow skills to strengthen, and green skills to enrich. A higher score reflects stronger development or management of the skill assessed.
On the Aggression and Shyness scales, interpretation is reversed: lower scores indicate stronger management, while higher scores suggest greater risk and warrant observation and support.
The instrument assesses three major skill domains (personal development, interpersonal relationships, and life skills), each with specific sub-skills, plus interpersonal communication and adaptation to change.
